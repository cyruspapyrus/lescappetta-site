About the firm

Aviation Leadership Advisors advises food, retail, technology and investment firms seeking entry and growth in North American airports.

The firm concentrates on major gateway airports and large public development programs. Every engagement is led personally by Les Cappetta.

  • Concept placement

    Matching food, retail and service concepts with the right airports, terminals and locations, and positioning them to win.

  • Real estate development

    Planning, design and construction of concession programs, from the first proposal through opening day.

  • Alternative funding

    Sourcing capital beyond conventional lenders for airport and public development projects.

Les Cappetta

Founder and Principal, Aviation Leadership Advisors

Les Cappetta has been an executive with three of North America’s leading airport concession developers and operators, and has since advised a wide range of companies that have entered and grown in the airport channel.

At HMSHost, the industry’s largest operator, he managed development, design and construction, and governmental affairs. In 2007 he became chief executive of Creative Host Services, then a $125 million concessionaire, and led its growth into SSP America. Under his leadership the company opened more than 80 restaurants at airports including New York JFK Terminal 4, Phoenix, San Diego and Houston, and brought The Palm and Shake Shack to JFK. He later served as Chairman of SSP North America. The business now generates more than $1.2 billion a year.

He is a past Chairman of the Airports Council International–North America (ACI‑NA) Associates Board and represented the association’s business partners on its Board of Directors. He serves on executive, non-executive and advisory boards, and brings a background in banking and in hotels with Marriott.
